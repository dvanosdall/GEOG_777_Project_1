Nitrate-Cancer Spatial Analysis

GEOG 777 Project - Interactive IDW Analysis

About

What does this do?

This tool tests whether groundwater nitrate levels predict cancer rates across Wisconsin census tracts using spatial regression.

Analysis steps:

  1. IDW interpolation creates a continuous nitrate surface from well measurements
  2. Zonal statistics extract average nitrate per census tract
  3. OLS regression tests the nitrate-cancer relationship

How to use it

Step 1: Adjust the k parameter (distance decay coefficient)

  • k = 2 (default): Standard IDW
  • Higher k (3-5): More localized patterns
  • Lower k (1): Smoother surface

Step 2: Click "Run Analysis" (takes ~2 minutes)

Step 3: Review regression results and maps below

Distance Decay Coefficient (k): 2.0 | 1.0 (less weight to distance) 5.0 (more weight to distance)
